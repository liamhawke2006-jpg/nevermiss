The short version

How you opt in

Consent is obtained verbally during a live phone call with a member of our team — we ask directly whether we may send you a follow-up text, and we record your answer, the time, and who took it. You may also opt in by texting our number first. We do not buy lists, and we do not send messages to anyone who has not agreed.

How you opt out

Reply STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT to any message. You will receive one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and then nothing further. Opting out is immediate and permanent, and it also cancels any message already scheduled. You can opt back in by replying START.
